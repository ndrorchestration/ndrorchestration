## Flagship research — Agentic Governance Orchestrator

An experimental evidence-bound agentic governance and orchestration system (historically DGAF), with a governance control-plane architecture and an evidence-bound Governance-as-Code implementation. The technical thesis is **epistemic transition control**: capability, evidence, verification, authority and permission are distinct machine-relevant states.

The existing repository includes explicit governance transitions, provenance binding, deterministic validators, negative controls, fail-closed CI, experimental custody and blinded-data tooling, content-addressed evidence receipts and presentation-only governance interfaces. Provider-neutral capability-governance reference work is bounded; broader durable, adaptive, interoperable orchestration remains a development objective, not a completed feature claim.

**Evidence before claims. Authority before action.**

- [Bounded public demo](https://project-7ybao.vercel.app/demo) — one governed action lifecycle; same-system engineering demonstration only.

### Research evidence and limitations

Track A Epoch 002 completed a preregistered blinded collection of 50 paired seed units / 2,250 observations, followed by governed dataset lock, bounded unblinding, materialization, locked primary-analysis execution, content-addressed result admission and bounded same-system interpretation. Closure is for its exact preregistered scope, not general efficacy.

A stronger conventional policy-as-code comparator (C3) matched 13/13 fixed fixtures and all 112,200 systematically enumerated bounded synthetic states with zero allow/deny differences. These are not independent real-world trials. Further bounded comparisons found no unique advantage under a neutral reusable-abstraction configuration/update model, in 11/11 composition/recovery cases, or in 8/8 provenance-custody mutation checks. These results reject unique-protection claims for the tested decision semantics; they do not establish equivalence of the full architectures. Incremental assurance-lifecycle value remains a question for fair comparative measurement.

## Evidence discipline and professional direction

Defined, implemented, tested, verified, independently verified, authorized, executed and empirically supported are distinct states. A passing test does not prove efficacy; a healthy deployment does not establish scientific validity; provenance does not guarantee truth; ability does not grant permission.

My professional focus is on systems that are easier to inspect, evaluate, constrain and verify, including AI evaluation, quality, prompt engineering, agentic assurance and provenance-focused systems work.
