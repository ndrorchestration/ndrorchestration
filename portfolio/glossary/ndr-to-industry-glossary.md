# NDR-to-Industry Vocabulary Glossary

**Purpose:** Translation layer between NDR internal terminology and industry-standard AI/ML vocabulary for use in job applications, client-facing materials, and public portfolio.
**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-07-02

---

## Usage Rules
1. Always use Industry Standard term in job applications, resumes, and cold outreach
2. NDR internal terms are for internal documentation, session logs, and Drive artifacts only
3. When presenting portfolio to technical hiring managers at AI safety orgs, NDR terms may be introduced AFTER industry term is established
4. CAaaS (Context Architecture as a Service) framing: retain for freelance/consulting pitches ONLY — remove from employment applications

> "Designed hierarchical context architecture to eliminate context drift across multi-agent workflow layers."
